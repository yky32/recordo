# App Store review notes (paste)

**Age: 4+.** No chat, no accounts.

Recordo is a **free** Hong Kong parking helper. No in-app purchases.

**User-generated content (Guideline 1.2)**  
Drivers may update a park’s **name, district, and hourly price**. Operator-official parks cannot have identity overwritten. Users can **Report** inaccurate name/price and **Hide** a park on-device. Reports land in `content_reports`.

**Location**  
When In Use only — to find nearby car parks.

**Notifications / Live Activity**  
Optional timer reminder to end parking and log the fee. Live Activity shows elapsed time on the Lock Screen. We do not send marketing notifications.

**Data sources**  
- Car-park tariffs we mark with a blue tick are copied from the mall/operator site.  
- Other prices are community reports, not official.  
- Space counts come from Transport Department participating car parks (`hk-td-tis_5`) — not counted by Recordo.  
- On-street meter bays and occupancy come from TD open data. Payment is via the official HKeMeter app.

**Screenshots**  
Use a real HK map (CWB/TST), official-tariff parks (blue tick), the timer, and meter dots. Do not present seed/demo prices as official.
